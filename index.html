1. Introduction

ARCHIVE / 000 respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Number Archive handles user information.

Number Archive is a single-player number collection game designed to work offline.

2. Information Collection

The current version of Number Archive does not require account registration or collect personal information directly from users.

The app does not require users to provide their name, email address, or other personal details to play.

3. Local Data Storage

Number Archive stores game progress locally on your device.

This includes collected numbers, game statistics, tickets, showcase configurations, and other gameplay information.

This data is not transmitted to servers operated by ARCHIVE / 000.

4. Data Export and Import

Number Archive provides optional features for exporting and importing game data.

Exported backup files are managed by the user. The developer does not automatically receive these files.

Users are responsible for storing and managing their exported backup files.

5. Advertising and In-App Purchases

The current version of Number Archive does not integrate third-party advertising services or process real-money purchases.

Advertising and in-app purchase features may be introduced in future updates. This Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect any changes to data processing.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress is stored locally on the user's device.

Users can delete locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage through their device settings. Uninstalling the app generally removes its locally stored data, although device backups or exported files may remain.

7. Children's Privacy

Number Archive does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data handling practices change.

Updates will be published on this page.
